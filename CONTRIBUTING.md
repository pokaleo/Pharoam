# Contributing to Pharoam

Thanks for your interest! Pharoam is in early development.

## What's open right now

- ✅ **Issues:** bug reports, feature ideas and content suggestions (pathways, skills) are very welcome.
- ✅ **Discussion** about the design in the [docs](docs/).
- ❌ **Outside pull requests are not accepted yet.** Contributor terms (a DCO or CLA) will be published before outside code is accepted ([Charter](docs/CHARTER.md) §7). Content contributions are expected to open first.

## How the project works

- Principles and design rules: [Charter](docs/CHARTER.md).
- Standards for code, tests, commits and PRs: [Engineering standards](docs/ENGINEERING.md).
- Instructions for AI coding agents: [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
