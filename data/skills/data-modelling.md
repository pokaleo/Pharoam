  "1": Follows an existing schema and adds simple tables or fields, with guidance.
  "2": Models entities and relationships for a typical feature, normalises sensibly, and chooses keys and constraints.
  "3": Designs data models that fit how data is queried and changed, balances normalisation against performance, and plans changes without downtime.
  "4": Designs data models for complex domains and many teams, across relational and other kinds of storage.
