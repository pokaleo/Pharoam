# Pharoam trademark and brand policy

Pharoam's code is open source. **Its name and brand are not.** This policy says how you may use them.

**Why it matters:** people trust Pharoam with an honest picture of their skills and, often, with their model keys. They need to be able to tell the official Pharoam from a copy.

## What this policy covers

The **Pharoam brand** means:

- the name **Pharoam**;
- the Pharoam **logo and wordmark**;
- the **Pharoam design system**: its visual identity, artwork and theme, including everything in `brand/`.

The Pharoam brand belongs to the Pharoam founder. It is not covered by the code licence ([LICENSE](LICENSE), AGPL-3.0-only), which grants no rights under trademark law ([LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)).

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to Pharoam truthfully**, in plain text: in articles, reviews, talks, teaching and comparisons; "works with Pharoam" for a compatible tool; "based on Pharoam" or "a fork of Pharoam" for a fork. Don't use the logo for this, and don't suggest that we endorse you.
- **Run the official, unmodified Pharoam app** for yourself or within your own organisation.
- **Keep the brand files, unchanged, in a copy of this repository**, for example a fork you use to propose changes back to us.
- **Link** to the official app or repository.

## You need written permission to

- use the name, logo or brand for a **modified version**, a **fork you share or deploy**, or a **service offered to others**;
- use "Pharoam", or anything **confusingly similar** (a near-identical name, a look-alike logo), in the name of a product, service, company, domain, app-store listing or social-media account;
- use the logo or any design-system artwork in any other way, **merchandise** included;
- copy or closely imitate the **look of the Pharoam design system** for another product;
- suggest that we **endorse, sponsor or are affiliated with** you.

**Never allowed:** using the brand to make a site or app look like the official Pharoam, for example to collect people's data or model keys.

## If you fork Pharoam

The AGPL lets you fork and share the code. To do that without using our brand:

1. **Choose another name** that is not confusingly similar to Pharoam.
2. **Delete `brand/`** and add your own artwork and theme. The app builds and runs without it.
3. **Replace the name** in the interface text (the translation files), page titles, app manifest and documentation.
4. You **may say**, in plain text, that your project is "based on Pharoam" or "a fork of Pharoam".
5. **Don't suggest** that your version is the official one.

Your fork still has the AGPL's obligations: for example, if you run a modified version as a network service, offer its users the source code.

## Asking for permission

Open an issue titled "Brand use request" that says what you want to use and how, or contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/pokaleo). Permission counts only when it is given in writing by the founder, and only for the use it names.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The version in this repository's `main` branch applies.
